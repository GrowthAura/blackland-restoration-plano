If you can see mold growth on more than 10 square feet of surface area, if mold is growing behind drywall or under flooring (discovered during a renovation or water damage repair), if there's a persistent musty odor you can't locate, or if you or family members are experiencing respiratory symptoms, headaches, or allergic reactions that improve when you leave the house, you likely need professional mold remediation. Why It Grows

Why Mold Grows in Plano Homes

North Texas Humidity

Collin County humidity runs above 60% from April through October, prime conditions for mold growth on any organic material (drywall paper, wood framing, carpet backing, insulation backing) that stays damp for more than 48 hours.

Hidden Water Leaks

The most common cause of mold in Plano homes is a water leak that went undetected: a slab leak under flooring, a slow supply line drip inside a wall, a shower pan leak rotting the subfloor, an HVAC condensate line dripping in the attic. The mold grows on the concealed side of materials, behind drywall, under flooring, on the back of cabinets, where you can't see it for weeks or months.

Inadequate Drying After Water Damage

Water damage that was 'cleaned up' with towels and fans but never professionally dried leaves moisture inside walls, under flooring and in subfloor. Mold colonizes those concealed surfaces within 48 hours. By the time you smell it or see it, the colony is established and remediation is needed. See water mitigation.

Poor Ventilation in Bathrooms and Laundry

Bathrooms without exhaust fans, or with fans that vent into the attic instead of outside, trap moisture in wall cavities, ceiling drywall and attic insulation. This chronic moisture feeds mold growth over time.

Texas Rules

Texas Mold Rules and How We Handle Them

Texas regulates mold work through the Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation (TDLR). Under Texas rules, a mold project covering 25 contiguous square feet or more must be assessed by a licensed mold assessment consultant, who writes a remediation protocol, and remediated by a licensed mold remediation contractor. The assessor and the remediator must be separate, independent companies.

What that means for you:

  • Smaller areas (under 25 contiguous square feet): we can remove and clean them following professional containment and cleaning practices.
  • Larger areas: we coordinate with an independent licensed assessor who writes the protocol, then carry out remediation according to it, followed by a clearance check.

We'll explain which path your project needs during the first visit.

Our Process

Professional Mold Remediation Process

01

Assessment and Moisture Source Identification

Before removing any mold, we find what's feeding it. FLIR thermal imaging and Protimeter moisture meters locate the active moisture source, a leak, condensation, humidity intrusion, so we can fix it. Removing mold without fixing the moisture source means the mold comes back.

02

Containment

The affected area is sealed off from the rest of the home with polyethylene sheeting and negative air pressure. HEPA-filtered negative air machines run continuously to prevent mold spores from spreading to unaffected areas through the HVAC system or air movement.

03

HEPA Air Filtration

HEPA air scrubbers filter airborne mold spores from the contained workspace during the entire remediation process. HEPA filters capture 99.97% of particles 0.3 microns and larger, mold spores range from 1 to 30 microns.

04

Mold-Contaminated Material Removal

Porous materials colonized by mold, drywall, insulation, carpet, pad, particleboard, are removed, bagged in the containment area, and disposed of. Non-porous and semi-porous materials (wood framing, concrete, metal) are cleaned and treated in place.

05

HEPA Vacuuming and Surface Treatment

All surfaces in the containment area, framing, subfloor, slab, remaining drywall, metal ductwork, are HEPA-vacuumed to remove settled spores, then treated with EPA-registered antimicrobial product.

06

Moisture Source Correction

The leak, condensation source or ventilation deficiency that caused the mold is corrected. This may involve coordinating with your plumber for pipe repair, your HVAC technician for condensate line service, or your roofer for roof repair.

07

Structural Drying

If any materials are still above their dry standard after remediation, LGR dehumidifiers and air movers dry them to target levels. Daily moisture monitoring confirms the environment is dry enough to prevent regrowth.

08

Reconstruction

Removed materials are rebuilt: new drywall, insulation, texture, paint, flooring, trim. See reconstruction.

Black Mold

Black Mold Removal in Plano

"Black mold" usually refers to Stachybotrys chartarum, a dark mold that grows on materials that stay wet for a long time, like drywall after a slow leak. Not every dark mold is Stachybotrys, and color alone doesn't tell you the type. Either way, the removal process is the same: contain it, remove affected materials, clean, dry and fix the moisture.

Lasting Fix

Fixing the Moisture So Mold Doesn't Come Back

Mold returns when the water source stays. Every project includes identifying the cause: a plumbing leak, a water heater or AC leak, a roof leak, poor ventilation, or a past water loss that wasn't fully dried. We'll tell you exactly what needs fixing and can handle the drying and repairs ourselves.

Kitchen sink cabinets are one of the most common spots. A slow leak at the drain or supply line soaks the cabinet floor and the wall behind it. We set up containment around the cabinet, remove the damaged cabinet base and drywall if needed, clean and dry the area, then rebuild.

FAQ

Mold Remediation FAQs

Can I remove mold myself with bleach? +

Bleach kills surface mold on non-porous materials (tile, glass, metal) but does not kill mold inside porous materials like drywall and wood. The mold roots (hyphae) penetrate drywall paper and wood grain, bleach can't reach them. The mold appears to die, then regrows from the roots within weeks. Additionally, bleach does not address the moisture source, and it does not capture airborne spores that spread during disturbance. Professional remediation is needed for mold on drywall, wood, carpet or insulation. Call (214) 833-4785.

Does homeowners insurance cover mold remediation in Plano? +

It depends on the cause. If mold resulted from a covered water damage event (burst pipe, appliance failure, water heater tank failure), mold remediation is typically covered under the same claim, though many policies cap mold coverage at $5,000 to $25,000. Mold from gradual leaks, deferred maintenance, or humidity issues is typically not covered. We document the cause and remediation scope in Xactimate for your adjuster. Call (214) 833-4785.

How long does mold remediation take? +

A single-room remediation (one bathroom, one closet wall) typically takes 3 to 5 days. A multi-room remediation involving extensive drywall removal, attic work, or whole-house HVAC cleaning can take 1 to 2 weeks. We give you a detailed timeline after the initial assessment. Call (214) 833-4785.

Is it safe to stay in my house during mold remediation? +

Typically yes, the containment and negative air pressure system prevents spores from reaching other areas of the home. If the mold is in the HVAC system, in multiple rooms, or if anyone in the household has severe respiratory conditions or immune compromise, temporary relocation may be recommended. We assess and advise on a case-by-case basis. Call (214) 833-4785.

I smell mold but I can't see it, where is it? +

Hidden mold grows behind drywall, under flooring, inside wall cavities, on the back of cabinets, and in attic insulation, places you can't see without opening up the structure. A moisture mapping inspection with FLIR thermal imaging can locate the moisture source and the likely mold location without demolishing your walls. Call (214) 833-4785.

Commercial Difference

Why Commercial Water Damage Is Different

Scale and Speed

A commercial pipe break can release thousands of gallons across thousands of square feet before anyone discovers it, especially on weekends and holidays when the building is unoccupied. The volume of water, the area affected, and the number of materials involved all exceed typical residential losses.

Business Interruption Cost

Every hour your business is closed costs money. Employees can't work, customers can't buy, tenants can't access their space. Our restoration plan is built around getting your business operational as fast as possible, we pre-schedule work around your operating hours when feasible, and run 24-hour drying operations when the loss demands it.

Multi-Party Insurance Coordination

Commercial water damage often involves the building owner's policy, the tenant's policy, and sometimes multiple tenant policies across affected units. We document each space's scope separately in Xactimate and work with every adjuster involved to keep the claim process moving.

Building Systems Complexity

Commercial buildings have fire sprinkler systems, centralized HVAC, elevator pits, electrical switchgear, data/server rooms, and shared plumbing risers. Water damage to any of these systems requires specialized assessment and often involves coordination with licensed contractors in those trades.

01

FAQ

Commercial Water Damage FAQs

My office building had a pipe burst over the weekend, how bad is it? +

It depends on how long the water ran. A supply line break running from Friday evening to Monday morning can release 5,000+ gallons into the building. The damage depends on which floor the break occurred on, what's below it, and whether the water reached electrical panels, server rooms or elevator equipment. Call (214) 833-4785 immediately, the sooner extraction starts, the less damage and the shorter the downtime.

Does my commercial insurance cover water damage? +

Most commercial property policies cover sudden and accidental water damage, pipe breaks, sprinkler discharges, appliance failures, and roof leaks from storm damage. Gradual leaks and deferred maintenance claims may be disputed. We document the cause, the scope, and the restoration in Xactimate format for your adjuster. Call (214) 833-4785.

Can you work after hours so my business stays open? +

Yes. We schedule extraction and drying equipment placement around your business hours whenever the loss allows. Drying equipment runs 24/7 but doesn't require anyone to be present. For losses that require demolition and reconstruction, we can often schedule noisy work before or after business hours.

How do you handle water damage in a multi-tenant building? +

Each tenant's space is assessed, documented and scoped separately. We coordinate with the property manager and each tenant's insurance carrier. Common areas (hallways, lobbies, shared restrooms) are documented under the building owner's policy. Individual tenant spaces are documented under their respective policies. Call (214) 833-4785.

Quick Answer

Does Water Damage Always Need Professional Restoration?

If water soaked into carpet pad, drywall, insulation or subfloors, yes. Surface drying leaves hidden moisture behind, and mold can start growing within 24 to 48 hours.

Causes

What Causes Water Damage in Wylie Homes

Wylie has grown fast, and most homes are newer two-story builds with attic HVAC units, upstairs laundry rooms and builder-grade plumbing. Add North Texas storms and properties near Lake Lavon and Muddy Creek, and these are the problems we see most:

  • Washer hoses and toilet supply lines that burst without warning
  • Attic water heaters and AC drain pans overflowing into ceilings
  • PEX lines splitting in exterior walls during hard freezes
  • Heavy rain pushing runoff into garages and under back doors
  • Slab leaks from shifting clay soil

First 48 Hours

Your First 48 Hours After Water Damage in Wylie

Hour 0 to 1: Shut off the water, cut power to wet rooms if it's safe, and call us. A crew is dispatched immediately.

Hours 1 to 4: We stop the source, map moisture with thermal imaging and extract standing water.

Hours 4 to 24: Wet pad, swollen baseboards and soaked drywall are removed if needed. Dehumidifiers and air movers are set up.

Hours 24 to 48: Daily readings track the drying. Antimicrobial treatment keeps mold from taking hold.

Storm & Flood

Storm and Flood Water Cleanup Near Lake Lavon

When heavy rain hits Wylie, low lots and homes near creeks and greenbelts can take on water fast. Outside floodwater is Category 3, so contaminated carpet, pad and drywall are removed, and every surface is disinfected before drying. We also tarp storm-damaged roofs to stop more water getting in.

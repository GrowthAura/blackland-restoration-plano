Quick Answer

How Do I Know If I Have Mold After Water Damage?

Common signs are a musty smell, dark spots on walls or baseboards, peeling paint and warped drywall. In Plano homes, mold often hides under sinks, behind baseboards and in attics.

The Right Way

Mold Remediation vs. Spraying Bleach

Bleach on a moldy wall can lighten the color, but it doesn't reach the roots inside drywall, wood or insulation. Scrubbing without containment can also spread spores to other rooms.

Professional mold removal in Plano TX works differently:

Why It Happens

Why Mold Grows So Fast After Leaks in Plano

Mold needs moisture, a food source (drywall paper, wood, dust) and warmth. Plano homes provide plenty of all three, especially during humid North Texas summers. After a leak, mold can begin growing within 24 to 48 hours.

Common hotspots we find in Plano homes:

  • Under kitchen and bathroom sinks from slow drips
  • Behind baseboards after a leak that was mopped up but never dried
  • Around toilets and shower pans from failed seals
  • In attics near water heaters, AC drain pans and roof leaks
  • Around HVAC air handlers and supply vents
  • Behind refrigerators and dishwashers
  • Inside walls after a slow pipe leak
Texas Rules

Texas Mold Rules and How We Handle Them

Texas regulates mold work through the Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation (TDLR). Under Texas rules, a mold project covering 25 contiguous square feet or more must be assessed by a licensed mold assessment consultant, who writes a remediation protocol, and remediated by a licensed mold remediation contractor. The assessor and the remediator must be separate, independent companies.

What that means for you:

  • Smaller areas (under 25 contiguous square feet): we can remove and clean them following professional containment and cleaning practices.
  • Larger areas: we coordinate with an independent licensed assessor who writes the protocol, then carry out remediation according to it, followed by a clearance check.

We'll explain which path your project needs during the first visit.

Our Process

Our Mold Remediation Process

01

Inspection and Moisture Source

We find the mold and, more importantly, the water causing it. Moisture meters and thermal imaging show how far it may have spread inside walls.

02

Containment

The work area is sealed with plastic sheeting and zipper doors. A negative air machine with HEPA filtration keeps air flowing into the containment, not out to the rest of the house.

03

Removal of Affected Materials

Moldy drywall, insulation, carpet pad and other porous materials are cut out, bagged inside the containment and removed.

04

Cleaning

Framing and hard surfaces are HEPA vacuumed, wire-brushed or sanded where needed, then cleaned with antimicrobial solutions. Encapsulation may be applied to treated framing.

05

Air Scrubbing

HEPA air scrubbers run throughout the job to capture airborne spores.

06

Drying and Clearance

The area is dried and checked. For larger regulated projects, a clearance assessment is done by the independent assessor before containment comes down.

07

Rebuild

Drywall, insulation and finishes are replaced.

Black Mold

Black Mold Removal in Plano

"Black mold" usually refers to Stachybotrys chartarum, a dark mold that grows on materials that stay wet for a long time, like drywall after a slow leak. Not every dark mold is Stachybotrys, and color alone doesn't tell you the type. Either way, the removal process is the same: contain it, remove affected materials, clean, dry and fix the moisture.

Lasting Fix

Fixing the Moisture So Mold Doesn't Come Back

Mold returns when the water source stays. Every project includes identifying the cause: a plumbing leak, a water heater or AC leak, a roof leak, poor ventilation, or a past water loss that wasn't fully dried. We'll tell you exactly what needs fixing and can handle the drying and repairs ourselves.

Common Case

Mold Under the Kitchen Sink

Kitchen sink cabinets are one of the most common spots. A slow leak at the drain or supply line soaks the cabinet floor and the wall behind it. We set up containment around the cabinet, remove the damaged cabinet base and drywall if needed, clean and dry the area, then rebuild.

FAQ

Mold Remediation FAQs

I found black mold behind baseboards after a water leak. What should I do? +

Don't scrape or disturb it. Keep the room closed off if you can, and call us. Mold behind baseboards often means there's more inside the wall.

Why does my house have a musty smell after a roof or pipe leak? +

A musty smell usually means mold or bacteria are growing somewhere still damp. A moisture inspection helps locate it before you open walls.

Is mold covered by homeowners insurance? +

Coverage varies. Mold that results from a covered sudden water loss is sometimes covered, often with a limit. Mold from long-term leaks or humidity is often excluded. Check your policy.

How long does mold remediation take? +

Small areas are often done in one to three days. Larger projects that need an independent assessment and clearance take longer.

Local Risks

Why Richardson Homes Face Unique Water Damage Risks

Richardson straddles both Collin County and Dallas County, and its housing stock ranges from 1960s ranch homes to brand new Telecom Corridor condos. That mix creates water damage patterns you won't find in newer suburbs:

Aging Cast Iron Drains in Heights Park & Original Richardson

Homes built in the 1960s through 1980s in Heights Park, Reservation, Greenwood Hills and the original Richardson neighborhoods south of Arapaho Road often still have cast iron sewer drains under the slab. Cast iron corrodes from the inside over decades, eventually cracking or collapsing. When a cast iron drain fails under a slab, it produces a Category 3 sewage backup that requires PPE, removal of all affected porous materials, antimicrobial treatment and complete structural drying.

Canyon Creek & Prairie Creek: Large Homes, Complex Plumbing

Canyon Creek, Prairie Creek and Duck Creek neighborhoods have larger two-story and tri-level homes with multiple bathrooms, laundry rooms and HVAC zones. More plumbing connections mean more potential failure points. We frequently see water losses from failed washing machine supply hoses, dishwasher line connections and second-floor bathroom supply valves in these neighborhoods.

Telecom Corridor & Commercial Properties Along US-75

Richardson's Telecom Corridor along US-75 between Campbell Road and Spring Valley Road contains offices, data centers, restaurants and mixed-use buildings. Commercial water damage in these properties requires rapid response to protect equipment, inventory and business operations. We provide 24/7 commercial water damage restoration with priority response for business properties.

Spring Creek & Duck Creek Flooding

Richardson's northern boundary runs along Spring Creek, and Duck Creek cuts through the center of the city. Properties near these waterways, and in low areas around Collins Boulevard and Yale Boulevard, can take on storm runoff during North Texas flash flooding events. Storm water intrusion is Category 3 contamination requiring full sanitization protocols.

Slab Leak Water Damage in Richardson

Your plumber fixes the pipe under the slab, but the concrete, flooring and lower walls stay wet. We map moisture across the slab, lift or remove flooring when needed, and dry the concrete until it tests dry, so new flooring doesn't fail later. This is one of the most common reasons Richardson homeowners call us. See slab leak damage.

Sewage

Sewage Backup Cleanup in Richardson

Older cast-iron lines can crack, sag or fill with tree roots. When sewage comes up through a shower or floor drain, we contain the area, remove contaminated materials, disinfect every surface and control odors. Keep family and pets away from the affected area until cleanup is done.

Richardson Water Damage FAQs

My Richardson home has old cast iron drain pipes, should I be worried? +

If your home was built before the late 1980s and still has original cast iron drains under the slab, those pipes are approaching or past their expected lifespan. Signs of a failing cast iron drain include slow drains in multiple fixtures, sewage odor in the house, and patches of unusually green grass in the yard. A sewer camera inspection by a plumber can confirm the condition, and if the pipe does fail, we handle the Category 3 sewage cleanup and structural drying. Call (214) 833-4785.

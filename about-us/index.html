Our Name

Why We're Called Blackland

Plano sits on the Blackland Prairie, the dark expansive clay soil that runs through North Texas. That soil swells and shrinks with the weather, stresses plumbing under concrete slabs and shapes much of the water damage we see in Plano homes. Our name is a reminder that we know this ground and how local homes are built on it.

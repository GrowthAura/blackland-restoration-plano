Emergency Help

Call for Emergency Help, 24/7

Calling is the fastest way to get a crew moving. When you call, we'll ask:

  • Your address and the best number to reach you
  • What happened (pipe burst, appliance leak, sewage, storm, fire, mold)
  • Whether the water is still running
  • Which rooms or floors are affected
  • Whether you've contacted your insurance company yet

Then we'll dispatch a crew and give you simple steps to protect your home until they arrive.

While You Wait

While You Wait for Our Crew

01

Shut off water

Shut off the main water valve if water is still flowing.

02

Cut power safely

Turn off electricity to wet rooms if your breaker panel is in a dry, safe spot.

03

Stay clear

Stay away from sagging ceilings and sewage-affected areas.

04

Move valuables

Move valuables, electronics and documents to a dry place.

05

Document

Take photos and video of the damage for your insurance claim.

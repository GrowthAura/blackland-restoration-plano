Why Richardson Homes Face Different Water Damage

Slab Leaks

Slab Leak Water Damage in Richardson

Your plumber fixes the pipe under the slab, but the concrete, flooring and lower walls stay wet. We map moisture across the slab, lift or remove flooring when needed, and dry the concrete until it tests dry, so new flooring doesn't fail later. This is one of the most common reasons Richardson homeowners call us.

Older cast-iron lines can crack, sag or fill with tree roots. When sewage comes up through a shower or floor drain, we contain the area, remove contaminated materials, disinfect every surface and control odors. Keep family and pets away from the affected area until cleanup is done.

Can a House Be Saved After Flooding?

Quick Answer

Yes, most flooded Plano homes can be saved. Fast pump-out, removal of contaminated drywall and carpet, sanitizing and structural drying within 24 to 48 hours protect the framing and foundation.

24/7 Flood Cleanup in Plano After Heavy Rain and Flash Floods

North Texas storms can drop a lot of rain in a short time. When drainage can't keep up, water comes in under doors, through garages, up through floor drains and around foundations. Our flood cleanup Plano TX crews respond any hour with:

If your home sits near a creek, a low spot on the street, or the bottom of a slope, you already know how fast it can happen.

Category 3

Why Floodwater Is Always Treated as Category 3

Floodwater from outside is classified as Category 3, the most contaminated level. It can carry sewage overflow, lawn chemicals, animal waste, fuel and soil bacteria. That changes the cleanup:

Porous materials (carpet, pad, drywall, insulation) that touched floodwater are removed, not dried.

Hard surfaces are cleaned and disinfected.

Crews wear protective equipment and contain the area.

Belongings are sorted into what can be cleaned and what must be documented as a loss.

This is the biggest difference between a burst pipe and storm water damage in Plano. Clean water can often be dried in place. Floodwater can't.

Local Risk Zones

Plano Flood-Prone Areas: Spring Creek, Rowlett Creek and Low-Lying Streets

Plano has several creek corridors that rise quickly in heavy storms, including Spring Creek, Rowlett Creek and Russell Creek. Homes backing onto greenbelts, as well as properties in older East Plano neighborhoods with lower lots, tend to see more runoff and water intrusion.

Common calls we get:

Our Process

Our Flood Restoration Process

01

Safety and Pump-Out

We check for electrical hazards, then pump out standing water and extract floors.

02

Mud, Silt and Debris Removal

Silt is shoveled and extracted, and hard surfaces are pressure washed where appropriate.

03

Flood Cuts

Contaminated drywall is cut above the water line, typically at 2 feet or 4 feet depending on how high the water reached and how far it wicked. Wet insulation comes out with it.

04

Cleaning and Antimicrobial Treatment

Studs, slab and remaining surfaces are cleaned and treated with an EPA-registered antimicrobial.

05

Structural Drying

LGR dehumidifiers and air movers dry framing and concrete, monitored daily.

06

Rebuild

After drying is confirmed, our reconstruction team replaces drywall, insulation, flooring and trim.

Flood Insurance vs. Homeowners Insurance

This part surprises many Plano homeowners, so here it is straight:

Type of WaterUsually Covered By
Rising water from outside (surface flooding, creek overflow)A separate flood policy, such as NFIP or private flood
Rain entering through a storm-damaged roof or windowOften homeowners insurance
Sewer or drain backupOften requires a water and sewer backup endorsement

Every policy is different, so check yours or ask your agent. Whatever coverage applies, we document the loss with photos, moisture readings and an itemized estimate so your claim has what it needs.

FAQ

Flood Damage FAQs

What should I do first if flash flood water comes under my back door? +

Stay safe and avoid wet areas near outlets or cords. If you can safely reach your breaker, turn off power to affected rooms. Then call us so pump-out and sanitization can start.

Can flooded carpet be saved? +

Not after outside floodwater. Category 3 water contaminates carpet and pad, so both are removed and the slab underneath is sanitized.

How long does flooded home restoration take in Plano? +

Cleanup and drying usually take about one week. Rebuilding depends on how much material was removed.

Do you clean up mud after a flood? +

Yes. Mud and silt removal is part of every flood job, followed by disinfection of the affected areas.

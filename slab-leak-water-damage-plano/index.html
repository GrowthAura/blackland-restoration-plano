After the Repair

What Should I Do After a Slab Leak Is Repaired?

Quick Answer

Have the slab, flooring and lower walls moisture-tested and dried right away. In Plano homes, installing new flooring over a still-wet slab often leads to mold, cupping and failed adhesive.

Your Plumber Fixes the Pipe. We Dry Everything It Soaked.

A slab leak is a two-part problem. Plumbers repair the line, through the slab or by rerouting it. But by the time a slab leak is found, water has often been spreading under flooring and into wall bottoms for days or weeks.

If your plumber fixed the slab leak and you're wondering who dries the floors in Plano, that's us. We:

  • Map moisture across the slab, flooring, baseboards and lower walls
  • Remove or lift flooring that can't be dried in place
  • Dry the concrete slab with targeted dehumidification and air movement
  • Dry hardwood with floor mat systems when it can be saved
  • Monitor daily until the slab and surrounding materials reach dry targets
Why It Happens

Why Plano's Clay Soil Causes So Many Slab Leaks

Plano is built on Blackland Prairie expansive clay. That soil swells when it's wet and shrinks during drought, and North Texas swings between both. All that movement puts stress on water lines running under and through concrete slabs.

Other contributing factors:

  • Copper lines under the slab in many homes built in the 1970s through 1990s, which can develop pinhole leaks over time
  • Hot water lines expanding and contracting against concrete
  • Water chemistry and age wearing down pipe walls
  • Foundation movement shifting pipes at stress points

Concrete Drying

Drying a Wet Concrete Slab After a Plumbing Leak

Concrete holds water deep inside and releases it slowly. Wet concrete slab drying after a plumbing leak is usually done with:

Removal or lifting of flooring so the slab surface can breathe.

Low-grain refrigerant and desiccant dehumidifiers to pull moisture from the room.

Directed air movement across the slab.

Moisture testing at multiple points to track progress.

Laying new flooring on a slab that's still wet can cause adhesive failure, cupping and mold under the new floor. We confirm readings before anything gets installed.
Save Your Floors

Flooring Over Slab Leaks: What Can Be Saved

Hardwood

Cupped hardwood is common after slab leaks. If caught early, our hardwood floor drying system can often save it.

Tile

Tile itself survives water well, but the grout and setting bed may hold moisture. We test and dry through the grout lines, and remove tile only when needed for access or replacement.

Carpet

Pad usually needs replacement. Carpet can often be dried and reinstalled after a clean water leak.

Baseboards and Lower Drywall

Swollen MDF baseboards are typically replaced. Drywall bottoms are tested and dried or cut as needed.

Insurance

Does Texas Homeowners Insurance Cover Slab Leak Damage?

Many Texas homeowner policies cover the resulting water damage from a sudden slab leak, and some also cover tear-out and access, meaning the cost to break into the slab or floor to reach the pipe. The pipe repair itself is often treated differently. Coverage varies a lot by policy, so read yours or ask your agent.

We coordinate with your plumber's leak detection findings and document the loss with photos, moisture maps and an itemized estimate for your adjuster.

FAQ

Slab Leak FAQs

How long does it take to dry a slab after a leak? +

Most slabs need several days to a couple of weeks depending on how long the leak ran and how saturated the concrete got. Daily readings tell us when it's done.

Can I put new flooring down right after the slab leak is fixed? +

Not until the slab is tested dry. Installing over wet concrete is one of the most common causes of failed floors after a slab leak.

Is a warm wet spot on my floor always a slab leak? +

Not always, but it's a strong sign of a hot water line leaking under the slab. Have a plumber check it right away.

Do you rebuild after slab leak drying? +

Yes. Our reconstruction team replaces flooring, baseboards and drywall once everything is dry.

About Us

Why We Exist

Water damage doesn't wait for business hours, and homeowners dealing with it shouldn't have to wait either. We built this company around one idea: the faster a trained crew arrives with the right equipment, the less damage your home takes, the lower your repair cost, and the faster your life gets back to normal.

Every technician on our crew holds IICRC certification, the industry's recognized credential for water damage restoration, and follows the IICRC S500 Standard on every job. That means we measure moisture with FLIR thermal imaging cameras and Protimeter meters, we document every reading for your insurance adjuster, and we dry your structure to verified standards instead of guessing. What We Do

What We Do

We handle water damage restoration from the first emergency call to the final coat of paint:

  • Emergency Water Extraction: Truck-mounted and portable extractors remove standing water from floors, carpet, pad and wall cavities
  • Structural Drying: LGR dehumidifiers, high-velocity air movers, wall-cavity drying and hardwood floor mat systems dry the structure, not just the air
  • Mold Prevention & Remediation: Containment, HEPA filtration, antimicrobial treatment and moisture source correction
  • Sewage & Flood Cleanup: Category 3 water handled with proper PPE, demolition and sanitization protocols
  • Fire & Smoke Restoration: Soot removal, ozone deodorizing, structural cleaning and reconstruction
  • Contents Pack-Out: Photo inventory, professional cleaning, climate-controlled storage and pack-back
  • Full Reconstruction: Drywall, texture, paint, flooring, baseboards, trim and cabinets rebuilt to pre-loss condition

Insurance Help

How We Handle Insurance Claims

We know how stressful a water loss is, and dealing with insurance on top of it makes it worse. That's why we handle the documentation side for you:

  • Pre-work photography of every affected area before anything is touched
  • Daily moisture readings logged with calibrated meters throughout the drying process
  • Xactimate estimates, the estimating platform used by State Farm, Allstate, USAA, Farmers, Liberty Mutual, Travelers and virtually every carrier writing policies in Collin County
  • Direct adjuster communication, we answer their questions about the drying plan, equipment placement and scope of work so you don't have to play middleman

We'll also be upfront about what your policy is likely to cover and what it may not, so there are no surprises after the work is done.

Ceiling & Drywall Water Damage Repair in Plano, TX

Water stain spreading on the ceiling? Drywall bubbling, sagging, or dripping? Ceiling water damage means water is sitting above your head right now, in the attic insulation, between floor joists in a two-story home, or pooling on top of the drywall itself. Quick Answer

Can a Ceiling Collapse From Water Damage?

Yes. Water-soaked drywall gets heavy and can collapse without warning. If your Plano ceiling is sagging, bubbling or dripping, move everyone out from under it and call for controlled drainage immediately.

Warning Signs

Ceiling Water Damage Warning Signs

  • Water stain or discoloration, yellow, brown or dark ring on ceiling surface
  • Bubbling or blistering paint, water pooling between drywall and paint layer
  • Sagging drywall, weight of trapped water pulling drywall away from joists (COLLAPSE RISK, evacuate the room)
  • Active dripping, water dripping from ceiling, light fixture, or ceiling fan mount
  • Musty odor, hidden moisture above the drywall growing mold on joists or insulation

If you see sagging drywall, do not attempt to poke it or drain it yourself. The entire section can collapse, releasing dozens of gallons of water and heavy saturated drywall. Call (214) 833-4785.

Causes

What Causes Ceiling Water Damage in Plano Homes?

Attic Water Heater or HVAC Leak

The most common cause of ceiling water damage in Plano. Tank water heaters and HVAC condensate systems in the attic send water directly onto the ceiling drywall below when they fail or overflow. See water heater & AC leak damage.

Burst Pipe in the Attic or Between Floors

Supply lines and drain connections in attic spaces or running between the first and second floors can fail from age, corrosion, or Blackland Prairie clay soil shifting the foundation. Water follows gravity through insulation, joist bays and wall cavities to the ceiling below. See burst pipe water damage.

Second-Floor Bathroom or Laundry Overflow

A toilet overflow, tub overflow, washing machine flood or dishwasher leak on the second floor sends water through the subfloor to the first-floor ceiling. See toilet overflow cleanup and appliance leak damage.

Roof Leak from Storm Damage

North Texas hail storms and wind-driven rain can breach roofing, flashing and roof penetrations. Water enters the attic and runs along rafters and sheathing to the lowest point, often appearing as a ceiling stain far from the actual roof penetration point.

Dry or Replace

Can Wet Drywall Be Dried, or Does It Need Replacing?

It depends on three things: how wet it got, what kind of water it was, and whether the drywall still has its strength.

Usually dried in place

  • Clean water, caught quickly
  • Drywall that's damp but still firm
  • No sagging, no crumbling, no swelling at the seams

Usually removed and replaced

  • Ceilings that sagged or bowed
  • Drywall that's soft, crumbling or delaminating
  • Contaminated water (sewage, floodwater, or water that sat for days)
  • Drywall with visible mold growth

Paper-faced drywall is a food source for mold, so wet drywall removal and drying in Plano is often about preventing mold more than fixing the stain.

Our Process

Ceiling Water Damage Repair Process

01

Safety assessment

Electrical hazards near wet ceiling fixtures identified, breakers cut to affected circuits, collapse-risk sections identified

02

Controlled drainage

Small puncture at the lowest point of a bulging ceiling section to drain trapped water into containers, reducing collapse risk

03

Drywall removal

Saturated sections removed to expose the joist cavity and source above. Only damaged sections are removed, dry drywall is left in place

04

Source identification

FLIR thermal imaging traces the water path back to its origin (attic equipment, pipe, roof, upper-floor fixture)

05

Attic work

Wet insulation removed, attic deck and joists inspected and dried

06

Structural drying

LGR dehumidifiers and air movers dry exposed joists, framing, subfloor and remaining drywall. Daily moisture monitoring until all materials reach dry standard

07

Ceiling reconstruction

New drywall hung, taped, mudded and sanded. Texture matching to your existing ceiling pattern (knockdown, orange peel, smooth, popcorn). Paint matched and applied. See reconstruction.

Water Dripping Through Recessed Lights or Smoke Detectors. Water often finds the holes in your ceiling first, which means light fixtures and smoke detectors. Turn off the breaker for that circuit right away. Water and electrical fixtures are a dangerous mix. We'll dry the area and you should have an electrician check fixtures that got wet.

FAQ

Ceiling Water Damage FAQs

Is a water stain on my ceiling dangerous? +

A stain alone isn't dangerous, but it means water reached the drywall, and may still be sitting above it in the insulation or between joists. If the drywall is still firm and dry to the touch, the leak may have stopped, but the source needs to be found and the hidden space above needs to be checked for moisture and mold. If the drywall is soft, spongy or sagging, there's active water above it and a collapse risk. Call (214) 833-4785.

Can water-stained ceiling drywall be painted over? +

Only if the drywall is completely dry, structurally sound, and the source of the leak has been fixed. A stain-blocking primer is needed before paint, water stains bleed through regular paint. If the drywall is soft, warped, crumbling, or was wet for more than 24 hours, it should be replaced rather than painted. Call (214) 833-4785 for assessment.

How much does ceiling water damage repair cost in Plano? +

Cost depends on the number of rooms affected, whether insulation needs replacement, whether structural drying is needed, and the extent of drywall replacement and texture matching. A single-room ceiling repair from a small HVAC condensate overflow is significantly less than a multi-room attic water heater failure. We provide a detailed scope and Xactimate estimate before major work begins. Call (214) 833-4785.

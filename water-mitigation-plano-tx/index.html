What We Do

What Does Water Mitigation Include?

Quick Answer

Water mitigation in Plano includes stopping the leak, extracting standing water, removing soaked materials, applying antimicrobial and setting up commercial drying equipment, with daily moisture readings until the structure is dry.

What Water Mitigation Means and Why Every Hour Counts

Water mitigation is the damage-control phase of a water loss. It is not the rebuild. It is everything done to keep the problem from getting worse: stopping the source, removing water, pulling out materials that cannot be saved, and drying the structure.

Here is what happens when mitigation is delayed:

Time Since LeakWhat Is Happening
First hourWater spreads across floors, under baseboards and into carpet pad
1 to 24 hoursDrywall wicks water upward, particleboard cabinets swell, odors start
24 to 48 hoursMold can begin growing on wet organic materials
48 hours and beyondWood floors cup, subfloors weaken, and repair costs rise sharply

As an experienced water mitigation company Plano homeowners can reach any hour, we focus on getting through that first window fast.

Insurance Guide

Water Mitigation vs. Water Restoration: What Insurance Looks For

People use the terms interchangeably, but adjusters don't.

  • Mitigation is the emergency phase: extraction, removal of damaged materials, drying and moisture monitoring.
  • Restoration (or reconstruction) is the repair phase: new drywall, flooring, paint and cabinets.

Most homeowner policies include a duty to mitigate. That means you're expected to take reasonable steps to prevent further damage once you discover a leak. Waiting a week to call someone can give an insurer grounds to question part of the claim. Prompt emergency water mitigation Plano TX homeowners document properly is one of the strongest things you can do to protect your coverage.

Understanding the difference between water mitigation and restoration for an insurance claim also helps you read your estimate. Mitigation line items are usually billed first, and the rebuild follows once the structure is verified dry.

Our Process

Our 6-Step Water Mitigation Protocol

01

Stop the Source and Contain the Area

We isolate the leak, shut off the supply, and set up plastic containment barriers if the water is contaminated or if we need to protect unaffected rooms.

02

Inspect and Map Moisture

Thermal imaging and moisture meters trace water inside walls, under cabinets and beneath flooring. We record readings in a moisture map so we know exactly where to dry.

03

Extract Standing and Trapped Water

Truck-mounted and portable extractors pull water from carpet, pad, tile and hard surfaces. Extraction removes water far faster than any amount of air movement.

04

Remove What Cannot Be Saved

Wet carpet pad, swollen baseboards and saturated drywall are removed when needed. When we cut drywall, we use a controlled flood cut above the wet line so walls can dry from the inside.

05

Set Up Controlled Drying

Low-grain refrigerant (LGR) dehumidifiers, high-velocity air movers and, where needed, wall-cavity or floor mat drying systems are placed based on the moisture map. An antimicrobial is applied to affected surfaces.

06

Monitor Daily Until Dry

We check temperature, humidity (measured in grains per pound), and material moisture every day, and adjust the equipment until each material reaches its dry goal.

Preventing Secondary Mold Damage in Humid Texas Homes

Plano summers are humid, and that moisture in the air slows evaporation and keeps wet materials wet longer. Emergency water mitigation to stop mold growth works by lowering humidity inside the affected rooms below the levels mold needs. That is why we use commercial dehumidifiers instead of household units, and why we keep monitoring until readings confirm the structure is dry rather than just dry to the touch.

If mold has already started, our mold remediation team can step in under a separate containment plan.

FAQ

Water Mitigation FAQs

How soon should mitigation start after a plumbing leak? +

As soon as possible. Immediate water mitigation after a plumbing leak limits how far water travels and keeps more materials salvageable. We dispatch a crew right after your call.

Can I do water mitigation myself? +

You can mop up surface water and move belongings, but household fans can't dry wall cavities, subfloors or slab. Without moisture readings, there's also no proof for your insurer that the structure was dried.

How long does water mitigation take? +

Most residential mitigation jobs run three to five days of drying after the first visit. Hardwood, concrete and heavily saturated areas can take longer.

Will the mitigation company also do the repairs? +

We can. Once mitigation is complete, our reconstruction team handles drywall, flooring, paint and cabinets so you don't have to find a second contractor.

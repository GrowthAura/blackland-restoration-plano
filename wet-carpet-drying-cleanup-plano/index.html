Quick Answer

How Long Can Carpet Stay Wet Before Mold Grows?

Mold can start growing in wet carpet and pad within 24 to 48 hours. In humid Plano homes, fast extraction and drying of the carpet and slab is the best way to prevent it.

Our Method

Weighted Extraction for Drenched Carpet

A regular carpet cleaner wand barely touches a flooded carpet. We use weighted extraction tools: the operator stands on the unit and its weight presses down while it vacuums. That squeezes water out of the carpet and the pad beneath it, removing far more than surface extraction.

Flooded carpet water extraction in Plano is followed right away by drying, so the carpet doesn't sit wet.

Why It Matters

Why the Pad Usually Comes Out

Carpet pad is basically foam built to hold air, and it holds water just as well. On a concrete slab, which most Plano homes have, a wet pad traps water against the concrete where it can't evaporate.

When we pull back the wet carpet, remove the padding and dry the slab, a few things happen:

  • The slab can dry instead of staying wet under the pad
  • We can sanitize the slab and tack strip
  • Odor sources are removed
  • The carpet itself dries faster

New pad goes in once the slab is dry, and the carpet is reinstalled, re-stretched and seamed.

Drying Options

Floating Carpet to Dry It in Place

For clean water losses where the pad can stay, we sometimes "float" the carpet, pushing air underneath so the carpet lifts slightly and air moves across both sides. This works best in specific situations, and we'll tell you if it's the right approach for your floor.

Category 3 Water Means Carpet Disposal. If sewage, a toilet backup with waste, or outside floodwater touched the carpet, both carpet and pad are removed and disposed of. There is no reliable way to clean pathogens out of carpet fibers and pad. The slab is then disinfected.
Odor Control

Wet Carpet Smell and Mildew

If your soaked carpet smells like mildew after a leak, moisture is still trapped below. The smell comes from bacteria and mold growing in the wet pad and backing. Surface deodorizers won't fix it.

Our process:

  1. Extract remaining water
  2. Remove wet pad
  3. Treat the slab and carpet backing with an antimicrobial
  4. Dry carpet and subfloor with dehumidifiers and air movers
  5. Apply odor treatment once everything is dry
Prevention

Preventing Browning and Delamination

Wet carpet can develop brown stains as it dries, called browning, as minerals and fibers in the backing wick to the surface. Carpet left wet too long can also delaminate, meaning the backing separates from the fibers. Fast extraction and controlled drying help prevent both, and anti-browning treatment can be applied when needed.

Detail Work

Tack Strip Inspection

Tack strips along the walls hold your carpet in place. When they get wet, they can rust or rot. We check them during drying and replace any that are damaged before reinstalling.

FAQ

Wet Carpet FAQs

How long does it take to dry wet carpet? +

Usually two to four days with professional equipment. Carpet on a wet slab can take longer if the pad isn't removed.

Can I dry my carpet with a fan? +

A fan dries the top fibers. It won't dry the pad or the slab underneath, which is where odor and mold start.

Will the carpet shrink after it gets wet? +

It can if it isn't dried properly. Controlled drying and re-stretching during reinstall help prevent permanent shrinkage and wrinkles.

Do you replace the carpet pad? +

Yes. We remove the wet pad, dry the slab and install new pad before reinstalling the carpet.

The Science

What Is Structural Drying?

Quick Answer

Structural drying removes moisture from inside walls, studs, subfloors and concrete using commercial dehumidifiers, air movers and moisture meters. In Plano, it prevents the mold, warping and odors that fans alone can't stop.

Why Structural Drying Is Different From Running Fans

When water soaks into a home, most of it ends up inside materials: drywall, wood framing, insulation, the subfloor and the concrete slab. Box fans move air across the surface. They don't control humidity, and they don't push moisture out of a wall cavity.

Professional structural drying services in Plano work on three things at once:

  • Airflow across wet surfaces to speed evaporation
  • Dehumidification to remove that evaporated moisture from the air
  • Temperature kept in the range where drying happens fastest

Get the balance wrong and you can create condensation, warped trim, or a wall that feels dry but stays wet inside. Get it right and materials dry evenly, often without tearing anything out.

Save Your Walls

Drying Wet Walls Without Cutting Drywall

When water is caught early and it's clean, many walls can be dried in place. We drill small holes behind the baseboard and push dry air directly into the cavity with wall injection systems, so wet drywall and studs dry from the inside.

This approach to wall cavity drying in Plano TX can save drywall, paint and texture, which means less demolition and a smaller rebuild. If the water was contaminated, or drywall has lost its strength, we'll recommend a controlled cut instead.

Equipment

LGR vs. Desiccant Dehumidifiers

Dehumidifier TypeHow It WorksBest Use
Low-grain refrigerant (LGR)Cools air below its dew point to condense moisture, even at lower humidityMost residential losses, drywall, framing, carpet
DesiccantUses a moisture-absorbing wheel, works in cooler or very dry conditionsHardwood, concrete, large commercial spaces, winter losses

We choose equipment based on the materials and the conditions inside your home, not a one-size-fits-all setup. Air movers come in axial and centrifugal styles, placed to sweep wet surfaces at the right angle.

Deep Drying

Drying Studs, Insulation, Subfloors and Slabs

Wood Framing and Sill Plates

Wood studs and sill plates have dry targets based on the wood's normal moisture content. We measure wood moisture equivalent and keep drying until readings match unaffected framing in the same home.

Wet Insulation

Fiberglass batts that are only lightly damp may dry, but soaked or contaminated insulation is removed. Blown-in attic insulation is usually removed when it's saturated.

OSB and Plywood Subfloors

Upstairs leaks soak into the subfloor. Subfloor and framing drying in Plano often needs drying from both above and below.

Concrete Slabs

Concrete holds water and releases it slowly. Slabs may need several days of targeted drying, especially after a slab leak.

Timelines

How Long Does Structural Drying Take?

Most homes dry in three to five days. Every material dries at its own rate, so here's a general idea:

  • Drywall: often 2 to 3 days
  • Wood studs: 3 to 5 days
  • Hardwood flooring: 5 to 14 days with floor mat systems
  • Concrete slab: 3 to 10 days depending on saturation

So, how long does it take to dry wood studs after a leak? Usually three to five days with proper equipment, but we confirm it with a meter rather than assuming.

Daily Monitoring and Proof of Dryness

We visit every day to record temperature, relative humidity, grains per pound and material moisture. Equipment gets moved or removed as areas dry. When the job is complete, you get a record showing each area reached its dry goal, which is useful for your insurance claim and for any future home sale.

Moisture trapped behind baseboards, under cabinets or inside walls gets checked specifically, because those are the places that cause problems later.

Why DIY Drying Fails

  • Household dehumidifiers can't keep up with a full water loss
  • Fans alone push humid air around without removing moisture
  • Without meters, there's no way to know when framing is actually dry
  • Closing up a wet wall traps moisture and invites mold
Pairing industrial air movers and dehumidifiers for a flooded house with daily readings is the only reliable way to dry the structure.
FAQ

Structural Drying FAQs

Can you dry wet walls without cutting drywall? +

Often, yes. If the water was clean and the drywall is still firm, wall injection drying can dry the cavity without demolition.

Do you rent LGR dehumidifiers in Plano? +

We don't rent equipment out on its own. Our LGR dehumidifier drying in Plano includes setup, placement, daily monitoring and removal, because the equipment only works well when it's managed.

Is it loud to run drying equipment? +

Air movers and dehumidifiers make a steady hum. Most families stay home during drying and get used to it within a day.

What happens if the walls are closed up while still wet? +

Trapped moisture can lead to mold, odor, swollen trim and peeling paint. That's why we verify dryness before any reconstruction begins.

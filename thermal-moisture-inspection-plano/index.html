Quick Answer

How Do Professionals Find Hidden Water Damage in Plano?

Technicians use FLIR infrared thermal cameras to scan walls, ceilings and floors for cool spots, water evaporating from a surface drops its temperature 5 to 15 degrees below surrounding dry materials. The camera displays these temperature differences as color patterns, revealing wet areas invisible to the eye. Protimeter moisture meters then confirm and quantify moisture levels in drywall, wood, concrete and other materials without tearing anything open.

How It Works

How Thermal Imaging Finds Water

Wet materials are usually cooler than dry ones because water evaporating from a surface cools it. An infrared camera shows those temperature differences as color patterns, which helps reveal wet areas inside walls, ceilings and floors that look normal to the eye.

Thermal imaging water leak detection in Plano is a screening tool. A cool spot on camera could be water, or it could be a draft or missing insulation. That's why we always confirm with moisture meters.

Plano Homes

Why Plano Homes Need Professional Moisture Detection

Blackland Prairie Clay Soil Movement

Plano sits on Blackland Prairie expansive clay that swells when wet and shrinks when dry. This seasonal movement shifts foundations, stresses supply lines and drain connections inside walls and under slabs, and creates leaks that run inside wall cavities and under flooring for weeks before visible signs appear. A slab leak under tile or hardwood can run for a month before the water bill spikes or flooring starts to warp. See slab leak water damage.

Attic-Mounted Equipment

Plano homes commonly have water heaters and HVAC air handlers installed in the attic. A slow condensate drip or a water heater drain valve seep soaks attic insulation and ceiling drywall gradually, you don't see damage until the ceiling stains or sags. Thermal imaging catches this moisture long before visible damage appears. See water heater & AC leak damage.

Post-Repair Verification

After any water damage restoration, the most critical question is: is it actually dry? Moisture trapped in wall cavities, under flooring, or inside joist bays after a "completed" repair grows mold within 48 hours. A post-repair moisture inspection verifies every material has reached its dry standard before drywall is closed, flooring is replaced, and paint goes on. See structural drying.

Our Toolkit

Professional Moisture Detection Equipment

FLIR Infrared Thermal Camera

The FLIR thermal camera reads surface temperatures across walls, ceilings and floors in real time. A wet area behind drywall cools the surface through evaporation, appearing as a distinct cool zone on the thermal display. This screening tool covers an entire room in minutes, something you can't do with a handheld moisture meter alone.

Important: Thermal cameras are a screening tool, not a moisture meter. A cool spot on camera could be water, a draft, missing insulation, or an HVAC supply behind the wall. That's why every thermal anomaly is confirmed with a moisture meter before any conclusions are drawn.

Protimeter Pinless Moisture Meter

Pinless (non-invasive) moisture meters scan drywall, wood and tile surfaces for moisture without making holes. They read moisture conditions in the material to a depth of about ¾ inch. Used for rapid scanning of large areas, entire rooms, hallways, cabinet interiors, to map the boundary between wet and dry.

Protimeter Pin Moisture Meter

Pin meters insert two small pins into the material surface and measure electrical resistance between them, wet materials conduct more electricity. Pin meters give precise quantitative moisture readings that determine whether a material is wet, damp, or at dry standard. Pin readings are the documented proof that drying is complete.

Insulated Probe Meter

For deeper readings, inside wall cavities, under subfloor through a small hole, deep in wood framing, insulated probe attachments extend moisture measurement beyond what surface meters can reach.

Thermo-Hygrometer

Measures ambient temperature and relative humidity in each room. Drying calculations depend on these readings, the psychrometric relationship between temperature, humidity, and moisture determines how fast materials dry and when dehumidifier adjustments are needed.

When You Need a Moisture Inspection in Plano

Mystery Musty Smell or Warped Baseboards

A musty odor, swelling baseboards or peeling paint near the floor often means hidden moisture behind the wall or under the flooring. A moisture check can locate the source before mold spreads. See mold remediation.

Sound of Water in the Wall

If you hear water dripping in a wall but see no puddle, a supply or drain line may be leaking inside the cavity. Thermal imaging traces where the water is going so your plumber knows where to open up, one precise cut instead of exploratory demolition.

High Water Bill With No Visible Leak

A sudden jump in your water bill can point to a hidden leak, including under the slab. Moisture mapping helps narrow the search. See slab leak water damage.

After a Plumber Fixes a Leak

Once the pipe is repaired, you need to know how far the water traveled. A moisture inspection defines the dry-out area so nothing gets missed, closing up wet framing behind drywall is one of the main causes of mold.

Before Closing Up Walls After Water Damage

Before drywall goes back up after a water loss, a thermal camera and meter inspection confirms the framing, studs, insulation and subfloor are at dry standard. Closing up wet framing is one of the most common causes of mold growth in repaired homes.

Before Buying a Home

A pre-purchase moisture inspection can flag past or active leaks around bathrooms, kitchens, windows and ceilings. Water damage history that was painted over or covered up is invisible to the eye but shows on thermal and moisture readings.

FAQ

Moisture Inspection FAQs

Can thermal imaging see through walls? +

Not exactly. Thermal cameras read surface temperature, not what's behind the surface. But water inside a wall changes the temperature of the drywall surface through evaporation cooling, which the camera picks up as a cool zone. Moisture meters then confirm whether that cool zone is water, a draft, or missing insulation.

Will the inspection damage my walls or floors? +

No. FLIR thermal imaging and pinless moisture meters are completely non-invasive, no holes, no cuts, no marks. Pin meters leave two tiny pin holes smaller than a nail hole that are invisible once filled. The inspection is designed to find water without creating damage.

Can you tell if my walls are dry after a leak? +

Yes, this is one of the most important uses of moisture inspection. Pin moisture meter readings document the exact moisture content of drywall, framing, subfloor and other materials. When all readings are at or below dry standard for that material, drying is confirmed. We log daily readings during structural drying to prove the dry-out is complete.

Do you find the leaking pipe? +

We find where the water is, the moisture map shows the wet area, the likely direction of travel, and the probable source location. Your plumber uses our map to open up at the right spot instead of guessing. We don't do plumbing work, but our map saves your plumber time and saves you from unnecessary holes.

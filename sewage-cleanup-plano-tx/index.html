Safety First

Is It Safe to Stay Home After a Sewage Backup?

Quick Answer

Stay out of the affected area. A sewage backup in a Plano home carries bacteria and viruses, so children, pets and anyone with health concerns should avoid it until cleanup and disinfection are complete.

Keep Your Family Away From the Affected Area. Before anything else: keep children, pets and anyone with a weakened immune system out of the area. Don't run water or flush toilets if drains are backing up. Avoid touching sewage-soaked items, and don't try to vacuum it up.

Then call us. Raw sewage removal needs the right equipment and the right process.

Health Risks

Health Risks of a Sewer Backup

Sewage is classified as Category 3, or black water, and can contain:

  • Bacteria such as E. coli and Salmonella
  • Viruses, including hepatitis A
  • Parasites and fungi
  • Contaminated sludge that soaks into porous materials

That's why sewer backup restoration in Plano TX isn't a mop-and-bleach job. Porous materials that absorb sewage can't be reliably cleaned, and fumes and residue can linger long after the water is gone.

Older homes in East and Central Plano and around Downtown are more likely to have original cast-iron drain lines. A main sewer line backup there often shows up first in the lowest drain in the house, such as a shower or tub.

Does Insurance Cover Sewer Backups?

Many standard homeowner policies exclude sewer and drain backups unless you've added a water and sewer backup endorsement. Some policies have a separate limit for it. Check your declarations page or call your agent. Whatever applies, we document the loss in detail so you can file with confidence. If the backup also caused mold growth, that may be handled separately.

FAQ

Sewage Cleanup FAQs

Is it safe to clean up sewage myself? +

We don't recommend it. Sewage carries pathogens, and porous materials that absorbed it need to be removed safely. Bleach on the surface doesn't reach what's soaked in.

How long does sewage cleanup take? +

Extraction and removal are often done in one day. Drying and final cleaning usually take three to five days.

Can you sanitize floors after a cast-iron sewer backup? +

Yes. Tile and concrete can usually be cleaned and disinfected. Carpet and pad that sewage touched are removed.

Do you fix the sewer line? +

We handle the cleanup and restoration. Your plumber repairs or clears the line, and we can work alongside them on the same day.

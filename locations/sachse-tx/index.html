Common Problems

Water Damage Problems We See in Sachse Homes

Sachse sits between Lake Ray Hubbard and Lake Lavon, with many two-story homes built from the 1990s onward. Attic water heaters, upstairs laundry rooms and supply lines are the most common sources of indoor flooding here. Heavy North Texas storms also push runoff into garages and low lots, and shifting clay soil can lead to slab leaks under tile and wood floors.

First Steps

Emergency Water Damage in Sachse? Do This First

  1. Shut off the main water valve.
  2. Turn off power to wet areas at the breaker if it's safe.
  3. Keep away from sagging ceilings and sewage-affected rooms.
  4. Photograph the damage for your insurance company.
  5. Call us and a crew will be dispatched immediately.

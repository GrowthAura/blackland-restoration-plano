Quick Answer

Does Insurance Pay to Rebuild After Water Damage?

If the water loss is covered, most policies pay to restore your Plano home to pre-loss condition, including drywall, flooring, cabinets and paint, based on an approved estimate.

One Team

Why Should Restoration and Reconstruction Be Done by the Same Company?

When the same IICRC certified team that dried your home also rebuilds it, there are no gaps. The crew that removed the drywall knows exactly where the cuts were made, which walls had moisture, where the cavity drying equipment was placed, and which materials were saved vs. replaced. A separate general contractor walks into a demolition zone with no context, they don't know why three feet of drywall was removed instead of four, whether the framing behind it passed moisture readings, or whether the subfloor was dried or needs replacement. One team, start to finish, means nothing gets missed and nothing gets rebuilt over hidden moisture.

What We Rebuild

What Gets Reconstructed After Water Damage

Drywall and Texture

Water-damaged drywall is cut out 2 inches above the visible water line to ensure all wet material is removed. Reconstruction installs new drywall, tapes and muds the seams, and matches your existing ceiling and wall texture:

  • Knockdown, Most common in Plano homes built after 1990
  • Orange peel, Fine uniform bump, common in 1980s-2000s builds
  • Smooth finish, Level-5 drywall finishing, no texture applied
  • Skip trowel, Hand-applied, found in some West Plano custom homes

Texture matching is critical, a mismatched patch is visible every day. Our finishers match the pattern, weight and density of your existing texture so the repaired area blends seamlessly.

Flooring

  • Hardwood, Matched species, stain and finish to existing floor. New boards woven into existing layout. Entire room may need sanding and refinishing for uniform appearance. See hardwood floor water damage
  • Carpet, Replaced with matching or upgraded carpet and pad
  • Tile, Matched tile and grout color. Field tile replacements. Transition strips at material changes
  • LVP/Laminate, Replaced with matching or equivalent product

Cabinets

Particleboard and MDF cabinets that absorbed water are replaced, they can't be repaired. Plywood and solid-wood cabinets that were dried successfully are reinstalled. Cabinet replacement includes matching door style, finish and hardware to existing undamaged cabinets.

Paint

The entire affected wall or ceiling gets a full coat, not just the patched area. Water stains bleed through standard paint; every repaired surface gets stain-blocking primer before finish coats. Paint color is matched to your existing walls.

Trim, Baseboards and Crown Molding

Baseboards removed during mitigation are replaced with matching profile, height and material. Crown molding, door and window casing, and shoe molding are matched and installed.

Insulation

Wet attic insulation (fiberglass batts or blown cellulose) removed during drying is replaced with new insulation at the same or better R-value.

Room by Room

Kitchen and Bathroom Rebuilds After Major Leaks

Kitchens and bathrooms take the hardest hit from slab leaks, dishwasher leaks and toilet failures, and they're the most complicated rooms to rebuild. Cabinets, countertops, plumbing, tile and appliances all have to come back in the right order.

Claim Support

Working With Your Insurance Adjuster on the Rebuild

Rebuild estimates are written in Xactimate, the same software most insurers use, so line items line up with how your adjuster reviews them. If hidden damage turns up during the rebuild, such as rotted subfloor or damaged framing, we document it and submit a supplement with photos and explanation.

A few terms you may hear:

  • RCV (Replacement Cost Value): what it costs to replace the damaged item new
  • ACV (Actual Cash Value): replacement cost minus depreciation
  • Recoverable depreciation: the amount some policies release after repairs are completed

Every policy is different. We'll help you understand your estimate and what documentation your insurer needs to release payments.

Finishes

Matching Your Floors and Finishes

Continuous flooring is a common challenge in Plano homes, where hardwood or tile often runs through several rooms without a break. Patching one room with a slightly different product can look obvious. We document the layout so your adjuster can see the flooring is continuous, and we source the closest possible match.

Saved your floors with hardwood floor drying? We'll handle any refinishing once the wood has fully settled.

Code

Permits and Code

Most drywall, flooring and cabinet work doesn't need a permit, but some rebuilds do, especially when structural, electrical or plumbing changes are involved. Where City of Plano permits are required, the work is permitted and inspected as needed, and licensed trades handle the regulated work.

Options

Restoring to Pre-Loss Condition, or Upgrading

Your insurance claim covers returning your home to its pre-loss condition. Some homeowners use the rebuild as a chance to upgrade, for example from carpet to LVP or to new cabinets. We can separate upgrade costs from claim-covered work so the estimate stays clean.

FAQ

Reconstruction FAQs

How long does water damage reconstruction take? +

A single room (one bathroom, one bedroom wall) typically takes 1 to 2 weeks after materials are sourced. A multi-room reconstruction involving kitchen cabinets, flooring in several rooms, ceiling work and painting can take 3 to 6 weeks. We give you a detailed timeline before work starts. Call (214) 833-4785.

Does insurance cover reconstruction after water damage? +

Yes, if the water damage was covered under your policy, reconstruction to return the home to pre-loss condition is part of the same claim. This includes drywall, texture, paint, flooring, cabinets, trim and any other materials removed during mitigation. We document everything in Xactimate for your adjuster. Call (214) 833-4785.

Can you match my existing flooring and cabinets? +

In most cases, yes. We source matching hardwood species, tile, carpet and LVP from the same manufacturers and distributors. For discontinued products, we match as closely as possible and discuss options with you before ordering. Cabinet doors and finishes are matched to your existing kitchen or bathroom. Call (214) 833-4785.

What if my contractor dried the house but didn't rebuild it? +

This is common, many mitigation companies don't do reconstruction. We can pick up where your mitigation company left off. We assess the current state, verify that drying was completed properly (with a moisture inspection), document the reconstruction scope, and handle the rebuild and insurance coordination. Call (214) 833-4785.

Can I upgrade materials during reconstruction? +

Yes, many homeowners use reconstruction as an opportunity to upgrade flooring, cabinets or fixtures. Your insurance covers the cost of restoring to pre-loss condition; you pay the difference between pre-loss materials and the upgrade. We can quote both options. Call (214) 833-4785.
